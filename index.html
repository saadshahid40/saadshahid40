SWOT analysis

A reflection on my current abilities, areas for improvement, opportunities and external challenges as an aspiring Mechatronics Engineer.

S Strengths

  • Engineering knowledge (PO1 – 100%): Apply engineering concepts through robotic arm, Boe-Bot and TurtleBot3 projects.
  • Practical experience: Robotics, PLC, ESP32, IoT, MATLAB, Python and machine vision.
  • Communication (PO10 – 100%): Share ideas, explain project work and prepare reports and presentations.
  • Independent learning (PO12 – 100%): Learn new technologies and improve engineering knowledge independently.
  • Professional responsibility: Value punctuality, honesty, safety and feedback.
  • Determination: Try different approaches and learn from mistakes.

W Weaknesses

  • Problem analysis (PO2 – 0%): Improve identifying causes of problems and selecting solutions independently.
  • Engineering design (PO3 – 25%): Develop creative ideas and prototypes while considering cost and safety.
  • Tools and techniques (PO5 – 25%): Gain more hands-on experience with sensors, electronic components and industrial equipment.
  • Teamwork (PO9 – 16.67%): Improve handling disagreements and collaborating across engineering disciplines.
  • Project management (PO11 – 50%): Improve task organisation, progress monitoring and managing delays.
  • Learning organisation: Build a more consistent schedule for studying advanced technical topics.

O Opportunities

  • Further learning (PO5, PO12): Take courses and workshops in robotics, automation, AI, machine vision and embedded systems.
  • Industrial experience (PO5, PO6, PO9): Apply for internships, attend industrial visits and learn from experienced engineers.
  • Projects and research (PO2, PO3, PO4, PO11): Develop skills through my final-year project and additional practical projects.
  • Networking (PO10, PO12): Attend conferences and seminars and seek guidance from professionals and lecturers.
  • Career opportunities (PO6, PO8, PO12): Explore engineering opportunities in Malaysia and internationally.

T Threats

  • Rapid technological change (PO5, PO12): AI and new tools require engineers to keep updating their skills.
  • Industry competition: Competition for internships and graduate jobs, alongside expectations for practical experience.
  • Training costs: Expensive equipment and professional courses may limit access to further training.
  • Academic and project demands (PO11): Deadlines and project work may leave less time for additional learning.
  • Industry requirements (PO7, PO12): Engineers are expected to apply sustainable practices and maintain professional competence.
