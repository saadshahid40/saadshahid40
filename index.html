01 / ABOUT

A little about me

My background, engineering work and the areas I want to keep developing.

I am a Mechatronics Engineering student at Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU), Malaysia, with interests in robotics, embedded systems, control engineering and machine vision.

My experience includes a robotic arm pick-and-place system, a Boe-Bot robot that followed a selected route and stopped when its ultrasonic sensor detected an obstacle, TurtleBot3 and ROS2 mapping and autonomous navigation, and a Smart Pedestrian Crosswalk prototype using ESP32 and Blynk IoT. I have also participated in PLC automation, AutoCAD modelling, MATLAB and machine vision projects.

My final-year project is A Deep Learning-Based Intelligent Vision System for Skin Ulcer Classification and Detection using U-Net. I aim to keep improving my technical knowledge, independent learning, critical thinking and professional skills.
